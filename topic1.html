Subtopic 1: IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS

There are three main ways cloud services are delivered to users:

  • IaaS: Renting physical or virtual servers and storage.
  • PaaS: A platform for developers to build apps without worrying about the underlying hardware.
  • SaaS: Software delivered over the internet like Google Docs or Microsoft 365.
